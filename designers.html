A process that cannot be replicated.

Scanography captures real flowers — arranged directly on a flatbed scanner at 1200 DPI — producing depth, texture, and color range that photography cannot achieve. Each composition is one-of-a-kind. No generative AI. No stock. The detail holds at 40×70″.

Available as ready-to-ship prints or custom commissions. Trade accounts receive wholesale pricing, priority turnaround, and a dedicated studio contact.
