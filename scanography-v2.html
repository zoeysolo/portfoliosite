How it works

01

Reserve your date

Reach out before your event to hold a commission slot. Wedding, memorial, graduation — any occasion works.

02

Ship your flowers

After your event, send your bouquet or arrangement overnight. Fresh or dried — both create different, beautiful effects.

03

Zoey scans & composes

Each bloom is arranged directly on the scanner glass and captured at museum-grade resolution. No post-processing tricks — just light, glass, and petal.

04

Receive your print

Delivered as archival giclée prints in your chosen size. Ready to frame. Yours to keep for a lifetime.

Weddings & Anniversaries

Bridal bouquets, ceremony florals, and first-anniversary flowers preserved as archival keepsakes.

II.

Memorial & Sympathy

Funeral arrangements and remembrance pieces transformed into lasting art. A different kind of grief work.

III.

Celebrations

Baby showers, graduations, milestone birthdays — any flowers from a day worth remembering.

Personal Gardens

Heirloom roses, a grandmother's peonies, a garden at the end of its season. Open commissions accepted year-round.
