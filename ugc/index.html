why scanner video works as an ad.

Scanner art already has a big audience on TikTok. No brand owns it as an ad format yet.

the hook is built in

The scan bar moves slowly across the glass and reveals the product line by line. People stay to see the whole image.

it reads as real

Scanner detail shows every vein, grain and speck of pollen. In a feed full of AI imagery, it is obviously made from the real thing.

the product is the star

No face on camera and no talking head. Your ingredients and your packaging carry the whole video, so it fits your brand look.

four formats.

Each one is built around the claim your marketing already makes: real ingredients, real flowers, real craft.

For fragrance

scan the notes.

Rose, vanilla pod, tobacco leaf, cedar shaving. Each note is laid on the glass and revealed in turn, then the bottle.

For tea

what's in the cup.

A blend poured onto the glass and scanned, then every leaf, flower and spice pulled out and named. Seasonal blends make a natural series.

For skincare and botanical beauty

from the field.

The fresh plant, then the same plant pressed and dried, then the product. Three frames, one story about where it comes from.

For florists and flower brands

one stem, one scan.

A single bloom, the scan bar sweeping across. Suited to weekly variety drops, and it outlasts the bouquet.

what every video includes.

Made for testing and reuse, so one shoot covers your feed, your ads and your product pages.

  • the finished editVertical 9:16, 10 to 20 seconds, ready to post on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
  • 2 to 3 alternate hooksDifferent opening seconds on the same video, so you can test what stops the scroll.
  • a clean versionNo text on screen, so your team can add its own copy, captions and offers.
  • full-resolution stillsThe scans themselves, sharp enough to print. No other UGC creator can hand you these.
  • usage rightsOrganic use on your channels included. Paid ad usage is priced by the 30 days.

how it works.

Short emails, one round of notes, files in a shared folder.

  1. brief

    Tell me the product, the launch or season, and where the videos will run.

  2. product

    Ship the product and ingredients to the Maryland studio, or I source them at retail.

  3. scan and edit

    Everything is scanned at high resolution and cut into vertical edits with alternate hooks.

  4. deliver

    Edits, clean versions and stills arrive in one folder. One round of revisions is included.

about the studio.

ZolomonPrints is a one-person studio in Maryland, run by artist Zoey Solomon. Every image starts with real material laid on the glass of a flatbed scanner and recorded in one slow pass of light. Whatever touches the glass comes out sharp, and whatever lifts away falls softly into shadow.

Zoey works across scanography, video, drawing and code, and sells signed archival prints of her scans. That practice is what goes into your videos.
