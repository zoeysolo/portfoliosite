01 / Overview
The Repositioned Thesis

ZolomonPrints is a luxury custom art studio specializing in heirloom-quality scanography prints made from clients' own botanicals — wedding bouquets, pressed flowers, meaningful objects. The primary sales driver is a curated B2B referral network of wedding planners, florists, and interior designers — not a marketplace algorithm.

Why This Positioning Works
  • Wedding bouquets are a perfect scanography subject — organic, sentimental, one-of-a-kind — and clients are already spending $5K–$30K on the day
  • Planners are a high-leverage sales channel: one trusted planner can generate 20–40 referrals per year with zero ad spend
  • White label lets venue and florist brands offer a premium product under their own name — expands reach without diluting ZolomonPrints identity
  • Interior designers as wholesale buyers create recurring, volume orders for residential and hospitality installs
  • Art licensing generates passive revenue from the same image library being built for commissions
The Scanography Advantage
  • No photographer can replicate the look — scanner produces a pitch-black ground with luminous, hyperreal botanical detail that reads as fine art, not a photo
  • Process is inherently bespoke — each scan is the client's actual flowers, irreproducible
  • Zero camera = zero setup fee optics; process mystique adds to perceived luxury
  • Archival inkjet printing on fine art stock supports "heirloom" framing — this is meant to last 100+ years
  • Founder's international exhibition history (Milan, London, Melbourne, Glasgow) provides credible fine art provenance for the luxury market

How We Reach Clients

Etsy is a discovery channel, not a sales engine. The business is built on relationships — with planners who trust us, designers who rely on us, and clients who become advocates.

Wedding planners, coordinators, and full-service venues are the highest-leverage sales channel. One trusted planner can reliably refer 10–30 clients per year. The 15% referral fee ($56–$75 per base commission) is meaningful income for planners without requiring them to inventory or manage anything — it's a pure value add for their clients.
Outreach Strategy
  • Identify 50–100 Philadelphia, DC, and mid-Atlantic planners/coordinators with luxury client bases — Junebug Weddings, The Knot Pro, and Instagram are primary research sources

  • White label option pitched to florists and venues who want to offer a branded keepsake product to their own clients

The Leverage Insight

At $800 average order value, ZolomonPrints needs 57 commissions to hit $42K — roughly one per week. A network of 5 active planners each referring 10–12 clients achieves that without a single ad dollar. Compare to Etsy's model requiring hundreds of transactions at $40–90 each.

Cost Structure
  • Fine art printing: ~$40–80/print depending on size (local fine art lab or archival POD)
  • Framing (when applicable): cost of goods ~$60–120; sold at $150–300
  • Planner referral fees: 15% of commission value (~$56–75/referral)
  • Packaging & shipping: ~$25–45/order; rolled into quote
  • CRM + studio tools: HoneyBook or Dubsado for inquiry flow (~$20/mo)
  • Marketing materials: sample prints for planner gifting, styled shoots
Margin vs. POD Model
  • Commission model at $800 AOV with ~75% margin = $600 retained per sale
  • POD model at $45 AOV with ~58% margin = $26 retained per sale
  • Commission model requires ~23× fewer transactions for equivalent profit
  • Framing markup is near 2× cost of goods — high-margin add-on worth prioritizing
  • Licensing and wholesale are near-100% margin on the image itself once built
