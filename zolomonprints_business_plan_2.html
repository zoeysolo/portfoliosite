01 / Overview
The Repositioned Thesis

ZolomonPrints is a luxury custom art studio specializing in heirloom-quality scanography prints made from clients' own botanicals including wedding bouquets, pressed flowers, meaningful objects. The primary sales driver is a curated B2B referral network of wedding planners and florists.

03 / Sales Channels
How We Reach Clients
Wedding Planner Referral Network
TypeB2B Referral
Commission15% of referred sale
White LabelAvailable
PriorityPrimary
Wedding planners, coordinators, and full-service venues are the highest-leverage sales channel. One trusted planner can reliably refer 10–30 clients per year. The 15% referral fee ($56–$75 per base commission) is meaningful income for planners without requiring them to inventory or manage anything.
Outreach Strategy
  • Identify 50–100 Philadelphia, DC, and mid-Atlantic planners/coordinators with luxury client bases
  • Personalized cold outreach via studio@zolomonprints.com and zoey@zolomonprints.com — sample print offered for their studio or styled shoot
  • White label option pitched to florists and venues who want to offer a branded keepsake product to their own clients
Florist Partnerships
TypeReferral + White Label
Commission15% referral or wholesale margin
PriorityHigh
Florists are the closest touchpoint to the bouquet itself, as they have the relationship with the client at the moment of maximum sentimental investment. Offering florists a co-branded or white label "preserve your bouquet as art" product is a natural extension of their existing services and a meaningful upsell.
  • White label tier: florist brands the product as their own; ZolomonPrints fulfills invisibly
  • Co-branded tier: "Created by [Florist] × ZolomonPrints" — mutual brand exposure
  • Florist receives 15% on referrals or negotiated wholesale margin on white label volume
  • Provide florists with a one-page client handout, postcard insert, or digital asset for their post-wedding follow-up workflow
Art Licensing

The scanography catalog being built for commissions doubles as a licensing library. Non-exclusive licenses for editorial, product, packaging, textile, and interior applications generate revenue from images that are already made
  • Platforms: Art Licensing Show, LIMA, Licensing International — register portfolio annually
  • Direct outreach to stationery brands, home goods manufacturers, publishers, and hospitality groups

The Leverage Insight

At $800 average order value, ZolomonPrints needs 57 commissions to hit $42K — roughly one per week. A network of 5 active planners each referring 10–12 clients achieves that.

Cost Structure
  • Fine art printing: ~$20–50/print depending on size (sourced by Artelo.io)
  • Framing (when applicable): cost of goods ~$60–120; sold at $150–300
  • Planner referral fees: 15% of commission value (~$56–75/referral)
  • Packaging & shipping: ~$25–45/order; rolled into quote
  • Marketing materials: sample prints for planner gifting, styled shoots
